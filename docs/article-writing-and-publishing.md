# Article Writing and Publishing

Use the personal `trading-article-writer` skill for every Chankay blog post draft or revision. This applies to both Technical and Trading articles. Read its Mermaid editorial guide whenever diagrams would help readers understand a comparison, sequence, or system relationship.

For Technical posts, use the skill's narrative, source-checking, diagram, and review workflow. Omit investment-only material such as trading rules, security selection, and the investment disclaimer. For Trading posts, follow the full skill, including its disclaimer format.

Give each diagram a question to answer and a nearby sentence explaining its takeaway. Keep diagrams legible in a narrow column, vary diagram families when useful, and label subjective coordinates as the author's qualitative judgment. Prefer the site's existing Mermaid Markdown support over separate image assets for software architecture and process diagrams.

Before changing production CMS content, present the complete localized article with its title, excerpt, slug, primary tag, and Markdown body. Publish only after the user explicitly approves that version. Preserve unrelated metadata and verify every published locale on the public site, including that Mermaid definitions render as diagrams rather than code blocks.
